03 · Two fronts under attack — and who’s attacking them

Capital is flowing at both ends of the market — vendors selling tools into practices, and apps organizing the consumer’s record. Every one stops at the same wall — and the two biggest are educating our market for free.

Front 01 · The small & mid-size practice

The vendors selling into practices.

ChartSpan

CLOSEST COMPETITOR

Self-described largest CCM provider in the U.S. — 100+ practices, ~$15M raised. A vendor: the practice still bills under its own NPI and carries the compliance burden. Medicare-centric. No consumer product.

HealthSnap

$48.5M RAISED

RPM + CCM service out of Miami. Same structure — billed under the provider’s NPI; the practice stays in the billing chain. No consumer side.

Signallamp Health

REVENUE-SPLIT MODEL

Embedded remote nurses — funded, in its own words, via profit-sharing in the practice’s CCM/RPM reimbursements. The exact split structure we refuse on Anti-Kickback grounds.

CareHarmony

VENDOR MODEL

Outsourced care coordinators as an “extension of the practice.” The practice bills; the practice owns the risk.

Cadence · Commure/Athelas

~$1B · ~$6B

The category’s funded leaders — built for large health systems and enterprise, not the 230K independent practices.

The wall: every one is a vendor inside the practice’s billing. None delivers under its own NPI. None ever touches the consumer.
Front 02 · The consumer’s record

The viewers — and the giants training consumers to use them.

National “Listen to your body” campaign; clinical-grade alerts projected to flag 1M+ undiagnosed Americans a year — each ending “talk to your provider.” Then Apple shelved its own orchestration subscription. Apple captures the signal — and stepped back from the layer that acts on it.

Tailwind · 800M+ users

Launched Jan 2026. 1 in 4 of 800M+ weekly users already ask health questions; records connect via b.well. By OpenAI’s own words, “not intended for diagnosis or treatment.” The largest consumer-education engine for connected health data ever shipped — and it stops exactly where InPursuit begins.

~$100M+ RAISED

The wall — and the gift: every one stops at the record, by design or by its own admission. The two biggest companies in tech are spending billions teaching consumers to connect their health data — then handing them to whoever can act on it. That action layer is the one InPursuit occupies.

05 · The specific market we’re going after

Not a new pool of money to win — a standing one no one is reaching. Continuous, permanent, fully-funded Medicare care-management revenue, payable on fee-schedule codes that exist regardless of any demonstration model.

CCM

Chronic Care Management

Ongoing management for patients with two or more chronic conditions.

RPM

Remote Physiologic Monitoring

Device-based tracking of vitals between visits.

APCM

Advanced Primary Care Mgmt

Bundled primary-care management — added by CMS in 2025.

RTM

Remote Therapeutic Monitoring

Musculoskeletal and respiratory therapy adherence.

BHI

Behavioral Health Integration

Collaborative management of depression and anxiety.

TCM

Transitional Care Management

Post-discharge coordination that prevents readmission.

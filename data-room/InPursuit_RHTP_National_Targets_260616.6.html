Strategic Supplement · RHTP National Targeting

Where we go next, and why.

The Rural Health Transformation Program is live in all 50 states — but the rollout is uneven. Our targets are the states where an open technology window meets a rural provider we can apply through.

AUDIENCE Internal · Investors VERSION [260616.6] STATUS Confidential Draft

$50 billion is moving to the states over five years — first-year awards averaged about $200 million each (a $147M–$281M range), and every dollar must be obligated by October 30, 2026. That deadline pressure is pushing money to providers now — but states are moving at very different speeds: some are taking applications today, others are still negotiating their CMS agreements. Targeting is about timing and access, not just size.

Tier 1 — Open now: active application underway
Tier 2 — Near term: window live or imminent
Tier 3 — Build pipeline: line up a rural lead first

State stages shift weekly; each opportunity is verified against the state's own posting before we act.

How We Choose

Four factors set the order: an open window, the right program (technology, not workforce-only), provider access (a rural in-state applicant or a direct-vendor lane), and rural density.

The play

Win the open application, work the near-term windows, build the pipeline.

Convert Mississippi now for proof and first revenue. In near-term states, pair with a rural in-state applicant — or bid directly where a state buys technology itself. In pipeline states, source in-state providers ahead of the windows. The constant: InPursuit is the named technology, a rural in-state provider is the applicant, and timing is everything.

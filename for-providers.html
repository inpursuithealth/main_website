InPursuit Reflect™

Contactless vitals. FDA-cleared. No wearable required.

InPursuit Reflect is a contactless vitals scanning solution with seven FDA-cleared determinations across two 510(k) clearances. Patients scan from a smartphone camera — no cuff, no clip, no patch required.

For Partner Network members, Reflect is the monitoring technology that powers RPM billing. It generates the physiological data streams CMS requires for CPT 99454 and 99457 — without the device logistics burden of traditional RPM kits.

The membership model

How the Partner Network works.

01

Practice joins

Sign a membership agreement. No fees. No software to install. InPursuit handles onboarding, credentialing support, and patient enrollment setup.

02

Patients enroll

Eligible Medicare patients in your panel are identified and enrolled in applicable CMS chronic care programs with your oversight and consent.

03

InPursuit delivers

Our care coordination team and technology infrastructure execute the monthly touchpoints, monitoring, and documentation CMS requires.

04

Revenue flows

CMS pays on the chronic care codes. Your practice receives its revenue share monthly — additive to your existing billing, zero disruption.

Anti-Kickback Safe Harbor

The InPursuit Partner Network membership structure is designed under the Personal Services Safe Harbor (42 CFR §1001.952(d)). Revenue sharing arrangements between InPursuit and partner practices are structured to comply with applicable federal anti-kickback statute safe harbors. All agreements are reviewed by healthcare regulatory counsel prior to execution.

CMS Chronic Care Programs

Six billing programs. One integrated infrastructure.

CCM · 99490 / 99439

Chronic Care Management

Monthly care plan management for patients with two or more chronic conditions. 20+ minutes of non-face-to-face time per month.

~$62 / patient / mo · CY2026 CMS rate
RPM · 99454 / 99457

Remote Patient Monitoring

Continuous physiological data collection via InPursuit Reflect™. Device supply, transmission, and 20-min monthly review.

~$120 / patient / mo · setup + review
APCM · G0556–G0558

Advanced Primary Care Management

Comprehensive, person-centered care management for patients with complex or high-need conditions. Three complexity tiers.

~$110 / patient / mo · high complexity
BHI · 99484 / G0570

Behavioral Health Integration

Integrated behavioral health services for patients with mild-to-moderate mental health conditions managed in primary care.

~$49 / patient / mo · CY2026 CMS rate
TCM · 99495 / 99496

Transitional Care Management

Post-discharge care coordination within 7–14 days of hospital or facility discharge. High and moderate complexity levels.

~$215 / transition · high complexity
AWV · G0438 / G0439

Annual Wellness Visit

Medicare Annual Wellness Visit — initial and subsequent. Preventive care plan, health risk assessment, and screening referrals.

~$174 / visit · initial AWV

Federal Program

Rural Health Transformation Program — $50 Billion

The Rural Health Transformation Program (RHTP) was established under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act as the largest federal investment in rural and independent healthcare infrastructure in a generation. The program is designed to modernize care delivery, expand chronic disease management capacity, and close the technology gap for independent and rural practices.

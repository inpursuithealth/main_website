Rights and documents

  • Governing documents: full rights, preferences, waterfall position, information rights, and transfer provisions are set forth in the Operating Agreement as amended by the Class C unit designation, together with the Subscription Agreement.
  • Advisory pool: the Company maintains an advisory membership pool of up to 5% of the fully-diluted capitalization, issued as Class C-1 non-voting profits interests that are junior and subordinate to the Class C Units and subject to vesting and forfeiture. The pool is included in the pre-money fully-diluted base, so its dilution is borne by the existing members and does not reduce a Class C investor's negotiated percentage for this offering.
  • Structure: the Company is an LLC and intends to remain one. The founder may elect to reorganize into a different structure at a later date; upon any such reorganization, Class C Units exchange into the corresponding equity of the successor entity under the Operating Agreement.
  • How to proceed: accredited investors may request the subscription package and full data room access at invest@inpursuithealth.com.

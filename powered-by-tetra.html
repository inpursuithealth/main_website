The infrastructure

Six coordinated layers.
One patient-centered infrastructure.

TETRA™ is not a single product. It's a coordinated infrastructure built from six specialized layers — each solving a different hard problem, all working together so that you, for the first time, sit at the center of your own healthcare.

i.

The data layer

The foundation. Normalizes, harmonizes, and orchestrates health data across every source — clinical records, wellness apps, wearables, labs, claims, and more — into a single coherent longitudinal record.

ii.

The exchange layer

Plugs into the national trusted exchange framework. Reads from every network, writes back to every network — bidirectionally, continuously, and through the standards-based infrastructure healthcare already trusts.

iii.

The intelligence layer

Orchestrates AI across your actual record. Retrieval-augmented, grounded in real data, consent-gated at every call. Not a model trained on your records — a system that reads from them only when you allow it.

iv.

The ownership layer

A zero-knowledge container where you hold the keys. InPursuit is structurally incapable of reading your data. Not a promise we made — a property of the architecture itself.

v.

The protection layer

Shields patient data from AI scraping, unauthorized access, and commercial exploitation. Continuous monitoring at every touchpoint, with defenses designed for threats that legacy healthcare IT was never built to face.

vi.

The guardian layer

A protectionary agent that watches the rest of the infrastructure. Enforces consent, monitors access patterns, and guards the integrity of every signal flowing through the system — always on, never asleep.

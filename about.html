About InPursuit Health

We Are InPursuit
of Better Healthcare

A veteran-owned healthcare technology company building the infrastructure that puts patients back in control of their health data — and their future.

Our Purpose

Built by people who believe
healthcare can be better.

Vision Statement
We are InPursuit of a world where health data belongs to the person it describes and works for them. One record they own, driving care and wellness that is proactive, personalized, and connected across every provider, payer, and setting. A Person-Centric Healthcare Ecosystem, finally real.
Mission Statement
InPursuit Health builds the healthcare data infrastructure that gives each person a complete record they own, governs how AI reasons over it, and turns it into better health at lower cost. We deliver that care and wellness directly to consumers and equip independent practices to deliver it too, connecting individuals, providers, payers, employers, and pharmacies on one interoperable foundation. Because results matter, and data should finally work for the people it belongs to.

Our Story

From the battlefield
to the healthcare frontier.

InPursuit Health was founded by veterans and data industry pioneers who have operated in the most demanding, security-sensitive environments on earth — including the U.S. Department of Defense, the Department of Veterans Affairs, and over 400 law enforcement agencies.

Our leadership team has been building enterprise data architecture since the 1990s, working across 30+ national governments and the world’s largest data providers. When we looked at American healthcare, we saw the same broken silos, buried data, and friction that we’d spent careers solving in defense and intelligence.

So we built the infrastructure layer — the healthcare data fabric that connects providers, payers, consumers, and pharmacies into one interoperable ecosystem. Not another layer on top of broken systems, but a new foundation underneath them.

Today, InPursuit Health is a recognized member of the CMS Health Tech Ecosystem, selected alongside companies like Apple, Google, Amazon, and Oracle. But unlike those companies, we didn’t build the silos. We built the bridge.

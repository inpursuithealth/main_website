01What happened

InPursuit Health has established its first benefits-broker distribution relationship. J. Krug — an established employee-benefits firm — will offer the InPursuit membership, co-branded as the J. Krug Health & Wellness App, across its employer client base as an employer-sponsored benefit.

02Why it matters
Employer-sponsored distribution. InPursuit reaches consumers through employers, not paid acquisition.
Household-level enrollment. Each sponsored employee brings an entire household onto the top tier.
Near-zero acquisition cost. Distribution rides J. Krug's existing, trusted employer relationships.
A channel, not a customer. The motion is replicable across the entire benefits-brokerage industry.
A second engine. Diversifies go-to-market beyond the provider channel anchored by Honeycomb.
Recurring by design. Sponsored memberships renew on the annual benefits cycle.

The Channel Strategy

From one broker to a national distribution strategy.

J. Krug is the first proof point of a repeatable, partner-led model that scales reach faster than capital.
01Why this model scales

The J. Krug relationship is not bespoke. Every benefits broker manages a book of employer clients and places sponsored benefits into them each year. The same membership and the same enrollment mechanics apply, unchanged, to the next broker — and the one after that. Each partner added contributes an entire book of employers, so reach compounds while acquisition cost stays near zero.
